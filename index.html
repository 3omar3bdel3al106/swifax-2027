A family business since 1986

Eng. Sherif Hassan Abd El-Khalek founded Swifax in 1986 with a passion for high-quality chocolate. From a small beginning to a trusted name across Egypt, we have always been driven by quality, innovation and bringing joy in every bite.

Today Swifax is still family-owned. We make Moltobella spreads, Swi Cook cooking chocolate and the ten-flavour Juna Spread line, and from luxurious pralines to rich spreads, every product reflects our care for taste and craftsmanship.

  1. 1986Swifax is founded

    The journey began with a passion for high-quality chocolate and a vision for excellence.

  2. 1990Our first praline line

    Eight praline flavours, crafted as premium gifts.

  3. 2000Chocolate spreads

    We added rich chocolate spreads in several flavours.

  4. 2010A wider range

  5. TodayServing all of Egypt

Certified food safety

Our Food Safety Management System is certified by UKS Egypt ISO Certifications, accredited by the Egyptian Accreditation Council (EGAC) under CAB # 012415, and recognised through the International Accreditation Forum (IAF) Multilateral Recognition Arrangement. That means global quality and food-safety controls across our whole product range.
